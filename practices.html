Practices

Three pillars, one operating philosophy

Regulatory design, programme delivery and institution-building — each practice sharpens the others.

Pillar I

Financial Market Infrastructure Consulting

We work with central banks, securities regulators, financial market infrastructures, Tier 1 banks and other key stakeholders on the design, operating model and delivery of the institutions through which money and securities move. This is strategy, regulatory-design and programme consulting; we do not advise on the purchase or sale of securities.

Financial Market Infrastructure

Post-trade specialist consulting — regulatory design for trade reporting and repo markets, clearing and settlement operating models, default management, compliance with the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMI) and T+1 readiness — grounded in clearing-house operating experience and delivered with AI-native methods. See our FMI thesis →

Financial Services Digital Infrastructure

Strategy for the data centers, connectivity, payment rails and core platforms on which modern financial services are built across the corridor.

Pillar II

Management Consulting

A sector-agnostic capability built on hard-won delivery experience: we lead the transformation programmes that reshape how organizations are run, and deliver the complex projects that bring those changes to life.

Organization Transformation

End-to-end leadership of enterprise change — operating-model redesign, restructuring, process improvement and the change management that makes transformation stick.

Project Delivery

Disciplined management of complex, multi-stakeholder programs from mandate to handover — scope, governance and execution that hold to time and budget.

Pillar III

Ventures

Where Nigeria's rules call for an institution that does not yet exist, we build it: we design the operator, assemble the consortium — a Nigerian delivery partner, proven infrastructure software under licence, institutional sponsors — and provide the founding management through to go-live.

Trade Reporting Infrastructure

A Nigerian-owned trade repository for over-the-counter derivatives — the institution the Derivatives Trading Rules already require — extended over time to adjacent reporting classes.

Post-Trade Platforms

Cloud-native collateral management, settlement and reconciliation services that turn today's house processes into financial market infrastructure.

Digital Infrastructure

The data centers, connectivity and power-to-compute capacity on which modern financial services run — pursued selectively, where it reinforces the core.

Ventures are built with partners under regulatory supervision and funded by their sponsors. Gideons River contributes design, programme office and founding management; it does not manage third-party funds or solicit investment.
